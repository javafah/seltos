🟩 BlockWorld 3D

A tiny Minecraft-inspired voxel sandbox. Explore a blocky island, break blocks, and place your own. Your world runs locally in this page.

Controls
WASD — move · Mouse — look around
Left-click — break block · Right-click — place block
1–6 / mouse wheel — select block · Esc — release mouse

No account or downloads needed. Best on a computer with a mouse and keyboard.
